## What changed

**Stage-level fallback (D01).** Code: `lib/enrichment/capabilities.ts`, `lib/services/fallback-orchestrator.ts`, `lib/enrichment/provider-outcome.ts`, `lib/enrichment/provider-results.ts`, `lib/providers/hunter-extra.ts`, and extended `apollo.ts` and `signalhire.ts`.

- **Stages covered.** Research (company identity, then missing details), Find people, Find emails and Check emails all run Apify first, then the providers that officially support the operation. Enrich runs every stage.
- **Company identity.** A name match is never auto-accepted. It is accepted only when the opportunity's own source names the same domain; otherwise a person chooses.
- **What is recorded.**
  - Every call is written to a ledger (`ProviderCall`) before it is made.
  - Calls are capped per run and per day, with free searches capped separately.
  - Calls are not repeated inside the freshness window.
  - Outcomes are kept in 16 distinct categories and shown per stage.
- **Controls.** Each operation can be switched off, and a switched-off operation is never done by another provider.

**Aliases (D02).** A domain is accepted only on official evidence (the company's own website or profile) plus a name match; everything else goes to review. A person's decision is final. Tests cover both a company legitimately using two domains and unrelated companies that share a label.

**Result reporting (D03).** `MAIN` counts the contacts stage. A regression test covers Apify finding nothing, Hunter succeeding, the run ending COMPLETED, and the email showing on the person.

**Identity gate (D04).**

- `checkIdentity` keeps identity, ownership and verification apart.
- A conflicting or low-confidence match is held on the company as a possible match. It has "It is them" / "Not theirs" actions (audited, final), and the next provider is still tried.

**Usable-address rule (D05).**

- `addressDecision` handles invalid, bouncing, suppressed, opted-out, stale, unknown, catch-all, role and off-domain addresses separately.
- Each person's decision and reason is shown in the enrichment panel.

**Adapters (section 6).**

- Replays of each vendor's documented examples run through the real HTTP layer (`tests/provider-replays.test.ts`).
- The replays found a real bug: a failed POST lost its HTTP status. It is fixed.
- A bounded smoke script, `npm run smoke:providers`, is free by default; paid calls are opt-in and capped at 5.
- The provider docs are updated: `docs/provider-contracts.md`.

**Mailboxes (section 7, D06).**

- **Sending and reading.** Per-workspace SMTP sending (TLS only, checked without sending), and Gmail and Microsoft 365 OAuth for sending and reading.
- **Capability and senders.** Send-only, receive-only and full are kept separate. There is a default sender, a per-sequence sender, and replies go from the thread's own mailbox.
- **Delivery safety.** An atomic send claim prevents double sends. Threading headers are set, and the provider's actual Message-ID is stored.
- **Sequences.** Queuing is idempotent per enrollment and step.

**Channels and calendar (section 8).** Microsoft 365 calendar is built: OAuth, free/busy, and event create, move and cancel. The WhatsApp Business path is unchanged and still passes its tests. CalDAV, personal WhatsApp pairing and LinkedIn automation are not built; reasons are under "Remaining differences".

**Product gaps (section 9).**

- **Web push.** Payload-less VAPID pushes, per-kind switches, allow-listed endpoints, once-only delivery.
- **Payments.** Razorpay Payment Links at the Plan row's price, with a signed, idempotent webhook. A mismatched amount is recorded and not activated. No tax is computed.
- **Lead Lens.** Bare-name search lists namesakes from the free searches and reveals only the one a person chooses.
- **Copy and links.** Stale in-app copy is fixed in the nav notes, the Support FAQ and the lead research panel. The lead page now links a company to Lead Lens.

**Parity (section 10).** `docs/parity-matrix.md` has 158 rows, built from the captured 2026-09-24 reference evidence. Three rows were updated after today's work (CH-01, N-03, LD-14). No row is marked "blocked" wholesale; 22 states were never observed in the reference and are listed for fresh access.

## Remaining differences (not implemented, deliberately)

- **CalDAV calendars.** Per-server discovery and app passwords; left out rather than half-built.
- **Personal WhatsApp pairing.** No official API.
- **LinkedIn automated messaging.** Needs partner API access. LinkedIn stays assisted.
- **Email delivery and read receipts.** There are no provider delivery webhooks, so email never reaches DELIVERED or READ. There is also no per-mailbox daily throttle and no Always CC.
- **GST invoicing and tax calculation.** Not implemented. Prices are whatever the Plan rows say.
- **PDF.** Standard fonts only: ₹ prints as "Rs." and non-Latin text as "?". Fixing it needs an embedded Unicode font and shaping for Indic scripts.
- **Reports and TeamCollab.**
  - No proposal PDF (it uses print).
  - TeamCollab dashboard and reporting are partial; see the parity-matrix items TC-02 and TC-07, plus the missing Radar "Act now" list and changes timeline.
  - The platform-wide India Demand Index is blocked: it needs a cross-customer dataset. Own-workspace demand is not equivalent, and no market statistic is fabricated.
- **CRM connectors** (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive). Additional scope; not built.
- **Lead Lens reading of bare text.** A two-word company name typed on its own is read as a person's name.
